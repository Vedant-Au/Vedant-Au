Before my MSc I spent nearly four years building two businesses in India: a gym I helped grow from 5 staff to **3 branches, 25+ people and 1,200+ clients**, and a **60-room student accommodation venture** I co-founded from scratch, which held 100% occupancy for consecutive years while the local market averaged 60 to 70%. Running operations taught me how to keep things working. I went back to study to learn why they stop working.

The number is rarely the hard part. The hard part is noticing what doesn't fit, and deciding what to do about it before the full picture arrives. Every project below states its question, what I found, and where the analysis is weak.

**Looking for:** business analyst, consulting, data and risk analytics, and operations or project management roles from January 2027. Eligible for the UK Graduate visa on completing my MSc, which allows full-time work for 18 months with no sponsorship needed.
